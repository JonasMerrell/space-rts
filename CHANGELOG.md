# Hohmann's Reach — patch notes

Newest first. The app shows these under PATCH NOTES and once after each update.
Format: a `## <version> — <YYYY-MM-DD>` line, then `- ` bullets in plain player language.
Every release adds an entry here (a unit test checks the newest one matches the release version).

## 0.8.8 — 2026-10-29
- Plan your own maneuver nodes for a ship still in orbit: it leaves on the burns you set.

## 0.8.7 — 2026-10-29
- Base research now costs energy only (the advanced tier is unchanged).
- Moons and asteroids yield more matter (moons 8 to 12, asteroids 5 to 8 base).

## 0.8.6 — 2026-10-29
- UPDATE takes you to the game's own download page.

## 0.8.5 — 2026-10-29
- Any ship parked at an empty world claims it, tankers included.

## 0.8.4 — 2026-10-29
- Matter output is no longer capped by your shipyards.
- Fixed fleets sometimes failing to form or move.

## 0.8.3 — 2026-10-28
- Enemy ships flying together show as one group with a ship count; tap it to see what's in it.
- The app tells you when a newer version is out.

## 0.8.2 — 2026-10-28
- Smoother map with many ships and long courses.
- Outposts hold only shipyards and defense platforms; solar panels are their one upgrade.

## 0.8.1 — 2026-10-28
- A full orbit only stops new ships being built there; any number of ships may still fly in.

## 0.8.0 — 2026-10-28
- New economy for new wars: a slower start, lower mining yields and pricier dreadnoughts.

## 0.7.6 — 2026-10-28
- Outposts: a construction ship coasting around the sun can found a station site in open space.
- The event log groups fleets into one line ("Fleet Alpha (5 ships) is in orbit of …").

## 0.7.5 — 2026-10-28
- Orbit limits: asteroids and outposts hold 7 of your ships, moons 12, planets 15.
- Big stacks of parked ships show as one token with a count; tap the world to open the ring.

## 0.7.4 — 2026-10-28
- The browser version runs faster.

## 0.7.3 — 2026-10-27
- The build queue scrolls instead of cutting off long queues.

## 0.7.2 — 2026-10-27

## 0.7.1 — 2026-10-27
- New art for the advanced stations and ships.

## 0.7.0 — 2026-10-27
- Advanced research tier: 15 new techs in five branches behind the base tree.
- Laser launch arrays, stargates, torpedoes, mass drivers, minefields, laser batteries.
- Cloaking, decoys and lidar sweeps: go dark, look like something else, or sweep for hidden ships.
- Bots research and use the advanced tier too.

## 0.6.4 — 2026-10-27
- World sheets and the map show when an orbit is a standoff or a battle.

## 0.6.3 — 2026-10-27
- Shipyards and upgrades build side by side; bigger labs run more research at once.
- Fuel depots refuel faster and share fuel fairly between everything parked there.

## 0.6.2 — 2026-10-27
- Bug reports stay private.

## 0.6.1 — 2026-10-27
- Change course in flight: plot a new course for a ship that's already flying.
- Intercept courses: send a ship after an enemy ship.
- Queue builds, upgrades, stations and research before you can afford them; they start when the resources arrive.
- Transfer-window finder: where a parked ship can go and when.
- Sensors report button with NOW / CONTACTS / LOG tabs.
- Hosts can delete a war; ended wars show a results screen; send a bug report or idea from the menu.

## 0.6.0 — 2026-10-27
- Fuel vs. time slider: burn more fuel to arrive sooner.
- New system layouts: rocky inner worlds, gas giants with moons further out, a denser belt.
- Separate MUSIC and EFFECTS switches, and an ENEMY PATHS switch; all remembered.
- ZOOM TO and SYSTEM VIEW camera buttons.
- Copy and paste in text fields.
- Reconnecting keeps you in your game.
